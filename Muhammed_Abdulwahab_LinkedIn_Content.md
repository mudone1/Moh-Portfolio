## About

I build systems that turn manual business processes into working software — AI automation,
backend systems, and full-stack applications.

Before writing production code, I worked in business development and field operations, which
shapes how I approach engineering: I start with the workflow and the bottleneck, not the tech
stack. That combination — business thinking plus full-stack engineering — is what I bring to
every project.

What I've built:

• TDIS Logistics — a full-stack operational dashboard for a travel/ticketing business, plus a
  separate Node.js/Playwright connector service that automates airline B2B portal balance checks
  (AES-256-GCM credential encryption, retry handling, scheduled sync), and a WhatsApp automation
  service for booking workflows.

• Globe-Tech — a grant/referral platform with token-based staff referral links, a multi-tier
  staff hierarchy, automated Google Sheets sync, automated email workflows (Resend), and a
  bank-data verification and payout-tracking system.

• Fudur — a full-stack mobility platform connecting riders and drivers, with transactional
  booking logic, realtime chat, push notifications, and a Safety Centre (trusted contacts, SOS
  alerts, trip sharing). Currently extending this as Fudur Automation, focused on AI-powered
  workflow automation for repetitive business processes.

I work primarily in the JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem — Next.js, Node.js, React — with
PostgreSQL and Firebase, and I use AI coding tools (including Claude Code) to accelerate
implementation while keeping direct ownership of architecture, integration design, debugging,
and testing.

Open to remote AI Automation Engineer, Backend Developer, Full-Stack Developer, or Solutions
Engineer roles with international teams.
